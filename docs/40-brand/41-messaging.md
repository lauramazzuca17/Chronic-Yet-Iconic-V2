## One-liner / elevator pitch
Make sense of your health trends.

## Audience
| Segment | What they need | What they fear/avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary: owner (Laura) living with POTS | Reliable logging + imports + clear BP/HR/med trends for care-team conversations | Data leaks; noisy UI; fake “diagnosis” |
| Secondary: Demo tester | Explore UI with isolated fake/empty data | Seeing owner’s real health data |

## Value propositions
| # | Value prop | Proof / feature that backs it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Log what matters for POTS in one place | Single log screen; BP+HR, meds, symptoms, etc. |
| 2 | Bring Apple Health (and CSV/XML) in without drowning in huge exports | Import + alternate CSV/XML path |
| 3 | See how meds and BP/HR relate over time | Analytics charts |

## Positioning
Against scattered notes, Apple Health alone, or spreadsheets: this app is the **personal bridge** between daily logging, imports, and appointment-ready trends — without clinician portals, social, or AI diagnosis.
